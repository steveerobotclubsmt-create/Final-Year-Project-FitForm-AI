Data (one person):
  round 1 (27 Oct): AI Camera front + webcam side, plastic bottle held flat      4902 crops (3773 empty, 1129 holding)
  round 2 (27 Oct): AI Camera front only, bottle                                 4654 crops (3524 empty, 1130 holding)

=== Model in use since 15 Nov (models/hand_holding.onnx) ===

  per picture 98.1 % (2900): dumbbell 96.9 %, empty fist 100 %, empty open 100 %,
                             empty hand while the other hand holds a dumbbell 93.5 % (right) / 100 % (left)
  per rep vote as in FitForm: holding 97.9 % (234 votes), empty 99.1 % (342 votes)
  bottle (rounds 1 + 2, also trained on - checks nothing was forgotten): 99.9 %, same as the old model
Compared with the previous model on the same round-4 pictures (tools/eval_hand_classifier.py, OpenCV DNN):
  previous: per picture 61.9 %, dumbbell 7.2 %, per rep 'holding' 3.8 % - nearly every dumbbell rep was

  new:      per picture 98.1 %, dumbbell 96.9 %, per rep 'holding' 97.9 %
Speed: 6.4 ms (previous) vs 6.5 ms (new) per hand on the Pi 5 (throttled to 1.0 GHz) - the same network.
Training: ~10.5 min per epoch on the Pi 5 CPU (~85 min for 8), Pi at 94 C without a fan.
